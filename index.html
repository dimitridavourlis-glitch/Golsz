About GOLSZ

Every athlete deserves a real plan.

Good sports guidance is unevenly distributed. If you're at the right club, in the right country, with the right coach, someone tells you what matters and when. If you're not, you find out too late.

GOLSZ gives any athlete access to structured, personalised guidance — regardless of where they live or who they know.

Our story

Founded in Nicosia. Built for the world.

GOLSZ is built by a small team in Nicosia, Cyprus, working with early athletes and their families. We also welcome conversations with people who invest in early-stage sport, careers and AI.
