About GOLSZ

Building the network sport hasn't had.

GOLSZ started with a simple observation: every other industry has a professional network built around how it actually works. Sport never has.

Mission

One trusted place to find your next opportunity.

Our mission is to give everyone in sport — athletes, coaches, scouts, agents, clubs, federations, and the professionals who run the business side of the game — a single, verified place to find their next opportunity and plan their next step.

Vision

A career in sport is a path, not a single peak.

We believe a career in sport shouldn't be treated as one moment — a draft, a trial, a transfer window — but as a path with many stages, from first contract to front office. Our vision is a world where every professional in the sports ecosystem has the same career tools any other industry takes for granted.

Why sport needs its own network

General networks weren't built for how sport hires.

Sport runs on trust, credentials, and relationships that generic professional networks were never designed to capture — verified playing history, federation affiliations, trial and transfer windows, and career transitions with no direct industry equivalent. GOLSZ is purpose-built around these realities, instead of asking sport to fit into a template made for other industries.
