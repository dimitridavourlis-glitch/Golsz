For athletes

For clubs & recruiters

From opening to shortlist to signed.

Join the waitlist

Message athletes directly to gauge interest before committing to a trial, and post your openings straight to the feed.

For agents

Features

Purpose-built tools for sport, not a repurposed job board.

GOLSZ isn't a generic professional network. Every feature is designed around the specific way athletes, clubs, and agents find and vet each other.

AI-powered matching

Matches people and opportunities on skills, goals, and career stage — not just keywords.

Verified profiles

Playing history, certifications, and affiliations confirmed — so trust is built in, not assumed.

Opportunity board

Playing, coaching, scouting, medical, marketing, and operations roles across the sports industry.

Discovery tools

Search, filter, shortlist, and compare prospects with tools built for real scouting workflows.

The feed

Job postings, combine announcements, and profile milestones — no reels, just relevant updates.

Direct messaging

Message athletes, coaches, and agents directly through their verified profile — no back channels needed.

About GOLSZ

Building the network sport hasn't had.

GOLSZ started with a simple observation: every other industry has a professional network built around how it actually works. Sport never has.

Our mission is to give everyone in sport — athletes, coaches, scouts, agents, clubs, and federations — a single, verified place to find their next opportunity and plan their next step, treating a sports career as a path with many stages, not one moment. Generic professional networks were never built to capture verified playing history, federation affiliations, or trial and transfer windows — GOLSZ is purpose-built around how sport actually works, instead of asking sport to fit a template made for other industries.
