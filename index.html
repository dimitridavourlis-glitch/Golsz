How it works

Where you are. Where you want to go. What to do next.

Most athletes get advice in fragments — a coach here, a forum thread there. GOLSZ puts your whole picture in one place and works out the path from it.

1

Build your Sports Passport

Your sport, position, club, level, school year, measurements, highlights and career history — the record everything else is built on. It's yours, and you can share it with a coach through a link they can open without an account.

2

Talk to AI Scout

Scout asks about your situation and what you're aiming for, then answers in the context of your actual Passport instead of giving generic advice. It tells you when it doesn't know something rather than guessing.

3

Work your plan

Your goal in your own words, the pathway that fits it, a development plan, target schools and clubs, and benchmarks you can retest to see whether you're actually moving.

A path to NCAA Division I is not a path to a European academy, and neither looks like a path to a professional contract. You tell Scout what you're actually chasing — in your own words — and the plan, the milestones and the targets are built around that goal, not a generic template.

  • Your goal, your words. Written on your Passport and editable any time. Scout won't quietly overwrite something you wrote yourself.
  • Pathway and milestones for NCAA, NAIA, JUCO, U Sports, academy, European club, semi-professional and professional routes.
  • Target schools and clubs with contact tracking and follow-up reminders, plus outreach emails Scout can draft for you.
  • Benchmarks you can retest — recorded with how they were measured, so a hand-timed sprint is never quietly compared against an electronic one.

What GOLSZ won't do

No invented numbers. No false promises.

Recruiting is full of people happy to sell you a guarantee. We'd rather be useful than impressive, so it's worth being blunt about the limits.

  • Scout says "I don't know." Where GOLSZ has no verified data for your sport, it says so and gives general guidance rather than inventing a requirement you might train against for months.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. No promises of scholarships, trials or contracts — from us or from Scout.
  • Not a medical service. Scout won't diagnose an injury, prescribe treatment, or give you a weight-cutting plan. It will tell you when to see a physio, a doctor or a dietitian.
  • Deepest for soccer and basketball today. Other sports work, and Scout will say plainly where its knowledge of yours is thinner.

About GOLSZ

Every athlete deserves a real plan.

GOLSZ started with a simple observation: the athletes who make it are rarely the ones with the most talent. They're the ones who knew what to do next.

Good guidance in sport is unevenly distributed. If you're at the right club, in the right country, with the right coach, someone tells you which showcase matters, what a college actually looks for, and when to send the email. If you're not, you find out too late — and talent is not the thing that decided it.

GOLSZ is an attempt to hand that guidance to any athlete with a phone. Not a promise that you'll make it, and not a shortcut — a clear picture of where you stand, an honest read on where you could get to, and the next concrete thing to do about it. We'd rather tell you something true and unwelcome than something encouraging and invented.

Our story

Founded in Nicosia. Built for the world.

GOLSZ is built by a small team in Nicosia, Cyprus — a growing hub for sport, tech, and finance at the crossroads of Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. The app is live and open: you can create a Passport and talk to Scout today. We're building it in the open with early athletes and their families, and the product deepens sport by sport as real, verifiable data goes in.

If you're an athlete, a parent, or a coach with a view on what's missing, we want to hear it — tell us. We also welcome conversations with people who invest in early-stage sport, careers and AI.
