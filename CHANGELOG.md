# Changelog

## 2026-11-06

- Schools & clubs, on the Plan page: when you add or edit a club or coach, anything missing or wrong is explained right under that field, a quick double tap on Add only adds it once, and an edit that can't be saved stays open with what you typed.
- Each club or coach's draft email now has a "Save draft" button and a line that says whether it is saved. If a save fails, your words stay where they are and the page tells you which one to try again.
- Deleting a club or coach still takes two taps, but the first tap now says on screen when its draft email will be deleted too, and a fast double tap no longer deletes anything.

## 2026-11-04

- The app now shows its loading screen, with a visible "Loading…" in your language, straight away while the rest downloads.
- The app's first screen and the home page open faster on a phone connection.
- Every "Loading…" state in the app is checked so it never shows made-up content while your data loads.
