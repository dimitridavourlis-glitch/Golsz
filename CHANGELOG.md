# Changelog

## 2026-11-14

- Ask Scout "What should I do today?" and its answer now comes from your own plan: it names the same steps due today and the same count for this week that Home shows, with a "From your plan" card under the answer.
- If Scout's answer mentions a date, a step or a number that isn't in your plan, the app shows what your plan says instead, so Scout never hands you a made-up step or deadline.
- When you've used today's Scout questions, Scout now tells you plainly, with the count ("0 of 3 left today") and when more come, instead of going quiet. The text box makes way for a card that says the same.

## 2026-11-10

- New athletes can now go from sign-up to a dated plan in one visit: after creating an account and filling in their Passport, they set a goal and get their first steps, each with a date.
- Home then shows the next step from that plan by name, with its date, and how many steps are due today and this week, so the first visit ends with something to act on instead of an empty screen.
- If someone skips setting a goal or comes back before finishing, Home shows a clear "set your goal" card to pick up where they left off.
- On the goal screen, people using a keyboard no longer see a browser outline around the whole screen; the field or button they are on is still outlined in gold.
- The "page not found" page now has proper side margins on a phone, so its text and buttons line up under the logo instead of touching the screen edge.
- The sign-up and log-in form is now one narrow column on a computer instead of stretching across the screen, each field has its name above it, and the small print is larger and easier to read on a phone.
- The website and the app now share the same logo lettering, the same gold button and the same dark background, so moving from the home page to sign-up no longer feels like changing product.
- The "page not found" page has the full menu, on a phone too, and its footer sits at the bottom of the screen.
- The sign-up screen no longer jumps a moment after it appears. The lettering used to load late and push "See plans" and the form around under your thumb; it now loads while the logo is showing, and the form keeps its place either way.
- On the home page the goal buttons line up with the rest of the page, and the small grey captions are larger and brighter. On the About page the five-step strip reads cleanly on a phone.

## 2026-11-06

- Schools & clubs, on the Plan page: when you add or edit a club or coach, anything missing or wrong is explained right under that field, a quick double tap on Add only adds it once, and an edit that can't be saved stays open with what you typed.
- Each club or coach's draft email now has a "Save draft" button and a line that says whether it is saved. If a save fails, your words stay where they are and the page tells you which one to try again.
- Deleting a club or coach still takes two taps, but the first tap now says on screen when its draft email will be deleted too, and a fast double tap no longer deletes anything.

## 2026-11-04

- The app now shows its loading screen, with a visible "Loading…" in your language, straight away while the rest downloads.
- The app's first screen and the home page open faster on a phone connection.
- Every "Loading…" state in the app is checked so it never shows made-up content while your data loads.
